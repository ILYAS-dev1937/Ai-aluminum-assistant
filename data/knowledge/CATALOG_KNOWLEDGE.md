```
1. FRAME ASSEMBLY (DORMANT)
   - Corners assembled by mitre cut (coupe d'onglet) with press-fit corner keys (36x14mm)

   - Mitre joint sealed with silicone type "Ordoflex 40" or equivalent
   - Frame cut lengths: Rail H+T = L+50; Montant dormant = H+50

2. SASH ASSEMBLY (OUVRANT)
   - Traverses (horizontal rails) cut at 90° and inserted into montants (verticals)
     by straight cut — traverses into montant grooves
   - Fixed by stainless steel screws in traverse alveovis
   - Top and bottom guides fitted on each montant
   - End caps (bouchons) on central uprights

3. CUT ANGLES AND DEBIT FORMULAS
   - Dormant (frame) profiles: CUT at 45° (mitre)
   - Sash profiles: CUT at 90° (straight)
   - Rolling track (774): CUT at 90°
   - External sill (775): CUT at 90°

   CUT LENGTH FORMULAS (examples — vary by configuration):
   - Rail haut/bas (dormant): L + 50
   - Montant dormant: H + 50

   - Traverse (ouvrant) 3-panel: L/3 - 7 to L/3 - 9
   - Traverse (ouvrant) 4-panel: L/4 - 15 to L/4 - 17
   - Montant latéral ouvrant: H - 68 (dormant 850/851) or H - 70 (dormant 951)

   - Chemin de roulement (774): L - 84 (2-rail, 85 Rubis) to L - 88 (3-rail, 952)

4. SEALING

   - Adjustable keeps (gâches 7025) placed in open dormant rails
```
